## Frequently Asked Questions

How can I order a customized cake?

Message MJ Cakees on WhatsApp or fill in the request form above. Share your idea and celebration details, and the team will get back to you directly.

Can I request a specific design?

Yes. Send a reference picture or describe the theme and colours you have in mind, and the design will be discussed with you.

Which flavours and sizes are available?

Available flavours and sizes are shared when you enquire. Tell us what you’d like and we’ll confirm what’s possible.

How far in advance should I order?

Please enquire as early as you can, and include your required date in your request. We’ll confirm availability for that date.

Do you deliver in Islamabad and Rawalpindi?

MJ Cakees serves Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Delivery arrangements are confirmed with you directly once you share your area.

How are prices determined?

Pricing depends on the design, size, and flavour you choose. Share your idea and budget, and the price is confirmed before your order is finalized.
